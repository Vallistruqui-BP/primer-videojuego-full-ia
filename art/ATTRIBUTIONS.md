# Office props — original art

Created for Pedro Vallarino's **Primer Videojuego Full IA** on 2026-11-19,
using project-authored procedural meshes in Blender. Original module authoring:
Codex acting on the project's authorized art task. No downloaded models,
textures, fonts, addons, or third-party artwork are used in these sprites.

The editable source and derivatives are authorized for this game's source and
distribution. No broader public license or third-party ownership is asserted.
Blender's software license is independent from the original artwork it renders.

Files: `source/office-props.blend`, `../scripts/blender_office_props.py`, and
`../game/art/office/{desk,desk_square,chair,door}.png`. The manifest records actual hashes
and render version. The script itself is the original construction recipe.

`project://` provenance identifiers in the manifest point to these local
project-owned files; they are deliberately not invented public source URLs.
